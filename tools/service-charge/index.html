Understand your service charge

Read the invoice, check the approved rate and raise a documented query.

What does it cover?

Indicative categories include common-area cleaning, security, lifts, landscaping, shared utilities, management and insurance. A reserve fund may cover major replacements. Your approved budget determines the actual scope. In-unit repairs, district cooling and master-community charges need separate verification.

Read a Mollak invoice

  1. Match building, unit, owner reference and billing period.
  2. Check chargeable area and AED/sq ft rate against the approved budget for the correct year. Recalculate area × annual rate and prorate only where applicable.
  3. Separate operating charges, reserve fund, master-community items and any VAT. Request the basis for each.
  4. Reconcile opening balance, receipts, credits, adjustments and late fees. Check that charges are not duplicated.
  5. Verify the payment beneficiary using official management contact details before paying.

Official RERA / DLD Service Charge Index — select the relevant project and year; a different building or budget year is not a like-for-like comparison. DLD / Dubai REST is the official starting point for Mollak information and authority enquiries.

Raise a dispute

  1. Collect the invoice, approved rate, area evidence and receipts.
  2. Send a specific written query to building management / the owners association manager and retain a reference.
  3. Ask for the approved budget and a line-by-line reconciliation; request written correction of errors.
  4. If unresolved, ask DLD / RERA through official channels which complaint route applies to your case. Seek legal advice before withholding payment or challenging enforcement.

This review checklist and escalation sequence are indicative practical guidance, not a determination of liability or a legal deadline.
