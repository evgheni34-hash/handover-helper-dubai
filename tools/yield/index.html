All default figures are indicative, invented planning assumptions, not market quotes or promised returns. Enter building-specific quotes. Holiday-home eligibility and permits must be confirmed with Dubai DET.

How the comparison works

Gross yield uses scheduled annual rent or nightly rate × 365 × occupancy, divided by price. Long-term collected rent deducts vacancy; management is charged on collected rent. Holiday-home platform and management percentages both apply to booking revenue.

Net yield is operating income after service charges, chiller and other annual costs, before mortgage payments, divided by price. Cash-on-cash is annual cash after amortised mortgage payments divided by down payment plus extra initial cash. Monthly cashflow is annual cash divided by 12; seasonality is not modelled.

Other costs should include maintenance, insurance, permits, utilities, cleaning and replacements as applicable. Enter furniture and acquisition fees in extra initial cash. This simplified model excludes appreciation, tax and sale proceeds.
