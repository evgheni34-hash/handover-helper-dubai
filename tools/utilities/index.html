Utilities, without the guesswork

Understand cooling charges and follow the move-in order for your building.

Why does a chiller bill arrive when the flat is empty?

Indicative bill structure: fixed capacity / demand charge + metered cooling consumption + account or meter charges + applicable tax and adjustments. Fixed charges may continue with no consumption under your contract. “Chiller-free” does not establish who pays every cooling cost: check your lease and building arrangement.

Check contracted refrigeration tonnes (RT), annual or monthly fixed rate, billed days, opening and closing meter readings, consumption unit and tariff. Ask for the underlying provider invoice and allocation method if management bills you. Do not assume RT capacity charges and consumption units are interchangeable.

Illustrative calculation only (indicative): 4 RT × AED 750 per RT per year ÷ 12 = AED 250 monthly fixed demand charge, before consumption and other charges. This rate is a community-reported example, not an official tariff: source discussion. Confirm your contract with the provider.

Self-serve move-in

  1. Ejari (tenants) — register the tenancy through DLD / Dubai REST. Owners start with title deed / accepted ownership documents instead. Confirm current Ejari fees in the official channel; no universal fee is assumed here.
  2. DEWA — use the premise number and tenancy or ownership route. For eligible tenants, Ejari integration starts the activation process; pay the deposit and verify activation.
  3. Chiller — confirm the building’s cooling provider, contract, deposit and any first-owner charges. Submit its required tenancy or ownership documents.
  4. Gas — where available, register with the building-approved supplier and book its safe connection appointment.
  5. Internet — confirm building coverage, contract term, installation and relocation charges before booking.

This order is indicative: arrange appointments in parallel where permitted, and confirm move-in permits, lift bookings and building access separately.
